## Full description

```
manejoai is the all-in-one app for property-services businesses -
painting, cleaning, maintenance, and similar trades.

Run the whole job from your phone:
- Track jobs from scheduled to completed, with photos from the field
- Create and send quotes, approve them into invoices with one tap
- Get paid faster with automatic payment reminders
- See what's overdue, what's owed, and who your best customers are
- Ask the built-in AI assistant a question or have it draft an invoice for you (Pro plan)

For your crew:
- Give field workers their own restricted login - they can submit job
  reports with photos and clock in/out, nothing else
- Review their reports and convert the good ones into real jobs in one tap
- See every crew member's hours by day and by week

manejoai also connects to Telegram, so job reports your team already
sends in a group chat get turned into real jobs automatically.

A free 14-day trial is included - no credit card required to start.
```
